Project overview

This project uses Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Data Modeling to analyze a realistic business scenario and turn raw data into a decision-ready analysis. It is being built as a hands-on practice project to demonstrate the skill in a real workflow, not just in theory.

Business problem

Leadership lacked a single, unified view of revenue, sales, orders, customers, and product performance across regions and time periods — making trends and outliers hard to spot.

Business questions

  • Which regions and products drive the most revenue?
  • How do monthly sales trends compare year over year?
  • Which customer segments contribute the most value?
  • Which products consistently underperform?
  • What operational or seasonal patterns affect order volume?
