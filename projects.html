Exchange Rate Fluctuation and Economic Growth in Nigeria
Completed

An ARDL bounds-testing study of how currency depreciation, inflation, and interest rates affect Nigeria's GDP growth using 44 years of macroeconomic data (1980–2024).

Business problem
Understanding what drives Nigeria's economic growth amid persistent currency depreciation.
Key result
Exchange rate depreciation, inflation, and high lending rates significantly reduce GDP growth (model R² = 0.87).

Customer Analytics Using Python
Completed

RFM segmentation and sales analysis of 9,994 retail orders using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.

Business problem
The business couldn't tell which customers actually drove revenue versus which were low-value or at risk of churning.
Key result
"Champions" are just 24% of customers but generate ~40% of revenue; two product sub-categories were found to be net-unprofitable despite steady sales.
